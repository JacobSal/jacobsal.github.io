About

A thoughtful human wanting to make a difference.

I completed my Ph.D. in Biomedical Engineering at the University of Florida in the Human Neuromechanics Laboratory. My doctoral work investigated how aging changes the neural and biomechanical control of walking. I gained experience in hypercomputing, signal processing, and machine learning methods for analyzing large-scale mobile brain/body imaging (MoBI) datasets, and I contributed to four papers on the neural control of human walking in younger and older adults.

I am now a director of simulations in the health professions department at Lake-Sumter State College. I'm currently interested in the implementation of AI agents and biomimetic robots in simulations for healthcare training.

I have a diverse skillset with experience in biomedical and chemical engineering, animal and human research, education, and college administration. I am happiest at the intersection of computational methods and research for the betterment of human health and knowledge.
